FONTLOG for Butlery Sans

Basic Font Information
Butlery Sans is a modified and renamed OFL 1.1 derivative using source material
from Mona Sans, Albert Sans and DM Sans. It is maintained for the Butlery
recipe and meal-planning application.

ChangeLog
0.626 — 2026-07-30
- Metadata-only release.
- Harmonised copyright attribution across all six styles and three formats.
- Updated internal version identifiers and fontRevision to 0.626.
- Consolidated the unmodified OFL 1.1 text and upstream copyright notices.
- Added licensing documents, a version file and an assets manifest to the
  app-ready bundle.
- No glyph outlines, metrics, spacing, kerning, substitutions, hinting or
  visual behavior changed from 0.625.

0.625 — 2026-07-25
- Added true Italic, Semibold Italic and Bold Italic styles.
- Preserved the approved Butlery roman styles and numeral system.
- Included Latin Extended-A and app-ready TTF/WOFF2 assets.
