## 3. What personal data do we collect?

We collect the following types of personal data when you use Butlery:

### 3.1 Information you provide directly

- **Account information:** Email address, password (encrypted) if you sign in with email, username, profile picture
- **Two-step verification (optional):** If you turn on two-step verification, we store a secret key that links your authenticator app to your account, and protected versions of your backup codes. The backup codes themselves are never stored in readable form.
- **Profile information:** Display name, biography, food preferences
- **Content you create:** Recipes, menus, shopping lists, comments, ratings
- **Social features:** Friends, shares, messages
- **Share links:** When you share a recipe or a shopping list with a link, or invite a friend with a link, we create a short link. It stores your user id, the link address (which also contains your user id) and when the link was created. The short link is deleted automatically after 14 days, and when you delete your account. Your short links are included in the data export. A link you have already sent cannot be taken back from the recipient.
- **Communications:** Messages you send to us or other users
- **Feedback:** When you send feedback in the app, we store what you write, the kind of feedback, your email address if you give it, and a screenshot if you attach one. We also store the last 20 screens and actions you used in the app before the feedback, with their times, and your platform, app version and time zone.

### 3.2 Information we collect automatically

- **Usage data:** How you use the app (which features, when)
- **Device data:** Device type, operating system, app version
- **Technical data:** IP address, connection type
- **Analytics data:** Usage statistics (only with your consent)
- **Notification records:** Delivery and engagement records for notifications — which notification was sent to you (or that you triggered to others), when, its delivery status, and whether you opened it
- **Import log:** When you import a recipe, we store your user id, the page address (without certain parameters that can contain sign-in details), the website's name and how the import went, for example which import method you used, whether it succeeded, how long it took and whether AI was used. We use the log to see which websites import works on and to fix errors. The log is deleted automatically about 30 days after the import, even if the account has already been deleted.

### 3.3 Information from third parties

- **Imported recipes:** Recipes you import from external websites
- **Sign-in with Google or Apple:** If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", we receive your name, your email address and your user ID at Google or Apple. See section 6.5.
- **The guest list during the test period:** If you have asked to try Butlery before the app is open to everyone, we put your email address on a guest list so that you can create an account. Only those of us who run Butlery can see the list, and we use the address for nothing else. It is removed once you have created the account and confirmed the email address. An invitation you do not use stops being valid after 30 days and is deleted automatically shortly after. If you want us to remove it sooner, write to integritet@butlery.se.

### 3.4 If you try Butlery as a guest

If you try Butlery without an account, we create a temporary guest account with no email address. What you save as a guest (recipes, menus, shopping lists, pantry and allergies) and your birth year and acceptance of the terms are stored the same way as for a regular account. We also count how many AI imports the guest account has made, because a guest can import three recipes with AI.

As a guest you cannot share, publish, chat, add friends or report content. If you create an account, everything you saved comes with you. If you sign out as a guest, the guest account and everything in it is deleted immediately. If you do not use the guest account for 30 days, it and everything in it is deleted automatically.

## 5. How do we use your personal data?

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

### 5.1 Essential features (always active)

- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate your identity
- Store and synchronize your recipes, menus and shopping lists
- Provide basic app functionality
- Security and protection against misuse
- Ask for a one-time code from your authenticator app when you sign in with two-step verification turned on, and let you sign in with a backup code if you cannot reach the app.
- Handle reports: the person who reported something, and the person whose content was removed or whose profile was hidden, get a notification and a notice in the app with the decision and the reason for it, and can appeal the decision in the app. If a moderator reviews a decision and changes it, both get a new notice.
- Calculating nutrition values for recipes on your phone, using the Swedish Food Agency's food composition database (licence CC BY 4.0) as the source. No data is sent to the Swedish Food Agency. If you choose which food an ingredient should count as, the choice is saved on your household, so everyone in the household sees it, including members who joined through a group, and everyone who may edit the household can change it. The choice is saved without recording who made it and is deleted when the household is deleted. If you enter your own amount for an ingredient, it is saved on your household in the same way, either for one recipe or for all recipes. For one recipe, the recipe's id is saved too, and the amount is included in the export of your data. The app does not record what you eat and has no personal goals or daily values.

### 5.2 Optional features (require consent)

**Analytics (if you have consented):**
- Understand how features are used
- Identify and fix bugs
- Improve user experience
- Measure app performance
- Count, anonymously and per day, which channel (for example Instagram or the newsletter) new users came from. The count holds no user ID and cannot be linked to you. It remains if you later delete your account.

**Marketing (if you have consented):**
- Send newsletters about new features
- Inform about updates
- Share recipes and tips

**Social features (if you have consented):**
- Share recipes with friends
- See others' recipes
- Comment and rate
- Send messages

**Push notifications (if you have consented):**
- Notify you about comments
- Inform about shares
- Remind about activity

**AI-assisted features (if you have consented):**
- Extract and structure recipes from photos and web links (OCR and text analysis)
- Suggest weekly menus and recipe enhancements

These features use AI (Google Cloud Vertex AI / Gemini, processed within the EU — see section 6). Content produced with AI assistance — such as a recipe read from a photo or a suggested menu — is generated to help you and is always presented for your review and editing before it is saved; you decide what to keep. In line with the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 50), we tell you here when you receive AI-assisted content. Butlery does not use AI chatbots, emotion recognition, or AI-generated media presented as real.

**Shared allergies within your household (if you have consented):**

If you choose to share your allergy list with your household, its members — including anyone who joins later — can see the allergies and dietary choices you have entered, so the weekly menu can be planned around them. Allergy information is health data and is therefore processed on the basis of your **explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a))**. Sharing is off by default, is per person, and can be withdrawn at any time, upon which the list is deleted immediately. The data never leaves your household, is never shared with third parties, and is not part of any public or aggregated figure.

**Your name on dishes in shared menus (if you have consented):**

If you turn on "Show my name on my dishes in shared menus", anyone who opens a shared menu sees your name under the dishes you made, with a link to your public profile. This applies to every shared menu your dishes are in, older ones too, and menus others have shared on. The choice is based on your **consent (Art. 6(1)(a))**, is off by default and cannot be turned on by anyone under 18. We keep the choice and the time you last changed it on your public profile, and both are part of your data export. If you turn it off, your name disappears within 30 minutes. No name is stored in the menus; a dish carries only your user id as its creator. Anyone in a menu can write dishes into it, so your id can end up on a dish you did not make. If you then tap "This is not my dish", we remove your id from that dish at once, and a moderator sees the report. The report and a text copy of the dish (its title and description, and whether the dish named you as its creator) are kept as the row on reported content in section 8 says.

### 5.3 Community ratings ("Butlery-betyget") — legitimate interest, not consent

When you rate a recipe (the "everyone" rating, 1–5 stars), that rating is combined
with the ratings of other users who hold the **same dish** into a shared community
average, shown as "Butlery-betyget." We identify the dish by a content fingerprint
of its title and ingredients — **no recipe text is shared, only the numbers.**

- **What is shown:** only a **combined average and a vote count**, and only once at
  least **five different people** have rated the dish. A single rating is never
  shown on its own; the displayed average carries no identity.
- **What is stored:** your star value linked to the dish fingerprint and to your
  account. This record is **pseudonymous, not anonymous** — it is your personal
  data and is included in your data export and erased when you delete your account
  or the underlying rating.
- **Your family's private ratings never feed this** — household/"family" ratings
  are kept in a separate place and are structurally excluded.
- **Legal basis — legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f):** improving recipe discovery
  and menu quality with a trustworthy shared signal. This does **not** require your
  consent, but you have the **right to object (Art. 21)**: deleting a rating
  removes its contribution immediately, and you may contact us to object.

### 6.5 Sign-in with Google or Apple

You can sign in with your Google or Apple account instead of email and password. It is optional, and email and password work as before.

**What we receive:**
- **From Google:** your name, your email address and your user ID at Google. Google also sends a link to your Google profile picture. The picture is not shown and is not copied to our database, but the sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) keeps the link with the other sign-in details for as long as the account exists.
- **From Apple:** your name (only the first time you sign in), your email address and your user ID at Apple. If you choose to hide your email address at Apple, we instead receive a special address from Apple that Apple forwards to your real address. We then never see your real address. Such an account is not joined to a Butlery account you already have with your real address; it becomes a separate account.

We fill in the name for you when you create the account, and you can change it before the account is created. If a Butlery account with the same email address already exists, we ask for your Butlery password once and then link the sign-in methods.

**What Google and Apple receive:** they see that you sign in to Butlery. Butlery sends them nothing else, except when we ask Apple to remove the link to your account (see below). Google and Apple are themselves controllers of your Google and Apple accounts and process the data under their own privacy policies ([Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)). They are not our data processors.

**Legal basis:** Performance of contract (Art. 6.1.b), because you have chosen to sign in that way.

**Transfer:** Sign-in goes through Firebase Authentication (Google LLC, USA), which is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (see 6.4). Google and Apple are themselves responsible for how they transfer data about your account outside the EU/EEA.

**When you unlink Apple:** If you unlink Apple under Account and security, Sign-in methods, on iPhone or iPad, we ask Apple to remove Butlery's link to your Apple account, as when you delete your account. On Android and the web you remove Butlery yourself under Sign in with Apple in your Apple account.

**When you delete your account:** If you signed in with Apple and request deletion in the app on iPhone or iPad, we ask Apple to remove Butlery's link to your Apple account straight away, before the deletion starts. If Apple does not respond, the account is deleted anyway. On Android and the web we cannot ask Apple to do this yet; there you can remove Butlery yourself under Sign in with Apple in your Apple account settings. If you signed in with Google, you can remove Butlery's access yourself in your Google account settings.

### 6.6 Security check in older app versions (Talsec)

Versions of the app for Android and iPhone built before October 29, 2026 contained a security check, freeRASP from Talsec (Lynx SFT s.r.o., Czech Republic). Each time the app started, it sent information about the app's and the phone's security status, a device id and the phone's IP address to Talsec. Talsec can work out your approximate location (country, region, city) from the IP address. Together with the IP address, the device id is personal data.

- **Purpose:** Detect tampered apps and phones to protect the service.
- **Legal basis:** Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f). You can object (Art. 21).
- **Talsec's own use:** Talsec also uses the data to improve its own products.
- **Storage and transfer:** Talsec keeps the data for about 12 months, on servers inside and outside the EU, including in the USA. The transfer outside the EU uses Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). [Talsec's data processing agreement](https://www.talsec.app/data-processing-agreement)
- **How it stops:** New versions of the app do not contain the check. Update the app and no more data is sent to Talsec.

## 11. Local storage and similar technologies

Butlery is a mobile application and does not use browser cookies. Instead, we use:

- **Authentication tokens:** Secure tokens managed by Firebase Auth to keep you logged in
- **Encrypted local database:** Your recipes and data are cached locally using AES-256 encrypted storage (SQLCipher) for offline access
- **Secure storage:** Sensitive data (encryption keys) is stored in platform-provided secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
- **Images waiting to be sent:** Images you take or choose while offline (food photos, comment photos, chat images and a profile picture) are kept temporarily in the app on your phone, without location data, until they have been sent or you throw them away. An image that has not been sent is thrown away automatically after 30 days. A chat image that is waiting appears to the recipient as an empty box until it has been sent. Other images are shown to others only after they have been sent.

You can manage data processing consent in: **More > Privacy and data > Consents**

## 12. Children and minors

Butlery is intended for users aged 15 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 15.

The 15-year age limit follows from Swedish data-protection law (the Data Protection Act, ch. 2 § 4), which sets 15 for information-society services with a social component. Butlery has social features (comments, sharing, groups, ratings) and is therefore subject to the 15 limit — stricter than the GDPR general age limit of 13 (Article 8), which applies to ordinary processing without a social component.

To enforce this age limit, we collect your birth year at signup and keep an age-verification record (evidence that the check was carried out). The birth year is treated as account information and stored on our server. Signups blocked because the person is under 15 are logged anonymously, without any data linking an individual to their age.

If you are a parent/guardian and discover that your child has created an account:
- Contact us at integritet@butlery.se
- We will delete the account immediately
