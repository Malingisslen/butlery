Delete your Butlery account

In the app

Go to More → Account & security → Delete account permanently and confirm.

Without the app

Send an email to integritet@butlery.se from the email address you use in Butlery and say that you want your account deleted. We verify that the account is yours and reply within 30 days.

What happens

You are signed out and the account is deleted after 7 days. If you sign in during those 7 days you can cancel the deletion or choose to delete right away. Once the account is deleted it cannot be restored.

What is deleted and what is kept

We delete all your data permanently 7 days after your request, except data in an open report case, which is kept until the case is closed, at most 180 days.

How long each kind of data is kept is listed in the Privacy Policy, section 8.
